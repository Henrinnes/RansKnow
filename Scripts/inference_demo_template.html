RansKnow — live extraction demo

Paste transcript text and see what the RansKnow pipeline actually extracts from it — running the real regex-based Knowledge Agent plus trained linear models, both reimplemented faithfully in JS and numerically verified against the original Python.

Read before trusting the trained-model numbers: the rule-based "Knowledge Agent" section below is deterministic and matches knowledge_agent.py exactly. The trained LogReg/SVM predictions further down are fit on the Knowledge Agent's own weak (regex-derived) labels — not yet checked against human-annotated ground truth (Phase 0.3 of the project's plan). Family classification in particular is currently weak (≤0.08 macro-F1 in real cross-validation) — treat those numbers as illustrative of the pipeline, not as a claim the models work well yet.
