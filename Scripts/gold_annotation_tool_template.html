Annotation guide

Ransomware-relevant

Mark Yes if the video substantively discusses a ransomware incident, group, tactics, or defenses — not just a passing mention in a longer list of unrelated topics. A 40-minute threat-intel roundup that spends 90 seconds on ransomware among ten other topics is a judgment call — use Uncertain rather than forcing it, and note why.

Family

Write the name as the video itself uses it (e.g. "LockBit", "LockBit 3.0", "Black Basta"). Leave blank if no specific family is named, even if the video is otherwise ransomware-relevant. Don't guess from context — if it's unclear which group, leave it blank and note the ambiguity.

Dominant tactic

Pick the MITRE ATT&CK tactic that gets the most substantive discussion, not just the first one mentioned. If the video is a broad incident walkthrough touching many tactics roughly equally, pick whichever gets the most airtime/detail, or leave as "not sure" if it's genuinely balanced.

Platform

The platform most central to the ransomware discussion. If multiple platforms are discussed with roughly equal weight (e.g. a cross-platform ESXi+Windows campaign), use "Multiple".

When to use Uncertain

Genuinely — mark it rather than guessing. Uncertain cases go to adjudication between annotators, which is more informative than a confident-looking but arbitrary call.

The Knowledge Agent's guess

Deliberately hidden until after you've made your own call, via the collapsible panel below the transcript. Check it against your own judgment afterward if you want, but forming your own opinion first is the entire point of this exercise — otherwise it's not independent evidence.
